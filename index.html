04 /Our story

From humble beginnings to a lifelong passion

A lifelong respect for quality furniture.

My journey in the furniture industry began in the late 1970s, growing up around our small family furniture factory. I helped with whatever tasks I was given, learning the trade hands-on as I grew and developing a genuine passion for the industry.

From an early age, I learned the value of craftsmanship, attention to detail and respect for quality furniture.

Over the decades, those early experiences developed into extensive knowledge of furniture manufacturing, warehousing, transportation and installation.

Today, that experience forms the foundation of TransNova Transport & Logistics Pty Ltd, delivering premium furniture with the care, precision and attention to detail it deserves.
